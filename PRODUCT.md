# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Nemo is building personal AI for people at home and, over time, families. Current Windows preview testing is for invited adults aged 18 or over.

## Product Purpose

Make personal AI approachable through guided setup, personal spaces and useful conversation context. The public website explains the current preview and distinguishes it from future work.

## Positioning

Nemo is independent software built around Hermes. Hermes provides the agent engine; Nemo adds the personal and family experience around it.

## Operating Context

The current preview runs on Windows and uses an eligible ChatGPT subscription. Public downloads and hosted phone access are being prepared. The website is a static HTML/CSS/JavaScript site in `docs/`, with a local preview at `/nemo/`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Current preview capabilities include personal conversation and Hermes memory. Relevant saved notes, conversation context and permitted tool results can go to ChatGPT. Calendar and Gmail are optional connections. Shared family planning, Child mode and dedicated home hardware remain future work; public child access is not open. Local AI models are deferred.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve Nemo's name, direct language and clear distinction between available capabilities and future vision. Design choices belong in DESIGN.md. The Home hero presents the interface clearly on continuous cream, without a domestic photograph behind it.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing website copy, fictional product illustrations, two explicitly imagined AI-generated editorial scenes, and the public source repository. Generated scenes are not customer evidence or product hardware renderings. No invented customers, testimonials, release dates or availability claims.

## Product Principles

- Explain preview eligibility and requirements before asking visitors to act.
- Distinguish current personal features from future family features.
- Explain provider data sharing plainly.
- Preserve private application data and hardware designs; publish only the website artifact.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable critical disclosures, keyboard access, visible focus, native disclosures and static/reduced-motion fallbacks. Product illustrations are inert and use fictional data.
