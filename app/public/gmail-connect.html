How should Nemo connect to Gmail?

Read only. Nemo cannot send or delete emails. Connecting Gmail does not enable AI access: you control that separately in Connections. Retrieved snippets can reach Hermes’s cloud AI when enabled.

Sign in securely with Google. Your password stays with Google.

One setup step on your PC

Phone sign-in needs a Google Web application OAuth client. A Desktop client works only for sign-in on the PC.

Register this exact return address in Google’s client settings:

Download the client JSON, then run in the Nemo folder:

node scripts/setup-gmail-web.mjs --client "C:\path\web-client.json"

Your existing connection is preserved. If Google rejects the private domain, a supported registered callback domain must be configured before phone sign-in can work.

What’s stored on your PC

Local cache

Sender, subject, date and a short snippet only. No bodies or attachments. If Gmail mirroring is enabled in Memory, these snippets also have searchable local GBrain copies. The snippet cache is protected for your Windows user. GBrain memory text and vectors are kept in a separate local database. Clearing the cache also removes its mirrored email memories and leaves your original Gmail messages unchanged.

Earlier chat answers, backups and copies already sent to the AI are outside this cache deletion.

Gmail is shared by trusted devices paired with this PC. Disconnecting removes its local tokens and synced snippets for all of them.
