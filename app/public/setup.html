Hermes, for your family

Your own
assistant.

Nemo connects your phone to Hermes on your family's computer. Each person has their own conversations, connected accounts and memories.

1. Connect your phone

On the family computer, open Nemo and finish setup. When phone access is ready, choose Show my phone invitation. Scan the code with your phone camera, check your name, then choose Connect my phone.

Your invitation works once and expires after ten minutes. Create a new one on the computer if you need another. The computer needs to stay awake and online.

Already using a private family connection?

Your existing invitation and connection still work. If your family currently uses Tailscale, keep it connected until phone access has been set up in Nemo.

2. Add Nemo to your iPhone

Open Nemo in Safari. Tap Share, choose Add to Home Screen, then Add. Open Nemo from its new icon. If it asks you to pair again, request a new invitation for this installation.

Notifications require the Home Screen app and your permission. Voice and interactive computer views require the app to stay in the foreground.

3. Connect your accounts

Connect Gmail with Google's own sign-in screen. Connecting an account, saving optional snippets and allowing those snippets in AI replies are separate choices.

Never enter your Google password into Nemo or share it in chat.

4. See what Nemo remembers

Open Memory to review small facts and their summary. Edit a fact, highlight summary text to correct it, or remove a memory. Calendar events stay separate.
