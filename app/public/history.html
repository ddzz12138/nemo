About chat storage and deletion

New chat transcripts are stored in your private Hermes profile. Nemo keeps a display copy. Deleting a native conversation removes both active copies and keeps a deletion record to prevent reimport. Saved Memory is separate. Migration backups and any legacy shared Hermes records are retained separately by the host.
