Ranade typeface

Ranade is designed by Eashna Ranade and distributed by Indian Type Foundry through Fontshare. It has its own font licence; Nemo’s MIT licence does not apply to it.

The website uses unmodified, locally served font files. Font binaries are kept out of Nemo’s public source repository. Obtain fonts and review the applicable licence directly from the distributor before reusing them.
