02

On this website

There are no accounts, working chat inputs, analytics scripts or advertising trackers in this website. The conversations and family examples are illustrations.

GitHub Pages hosts the site and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. Links to GitHub and other services take you to websites with their own privacy practices. Fonts are served from this site.

If you email us, we receive the details you choose to send and use them to respond. Please keep private conversation exports and account credentials out of public GitHub issues.

03

Inside the Nemo app

The app runs on a home computer. Its personal records can include:

  • Profile & device detailsRecognise a person and their signed-in devices.
  • Conversations & historyContinue chats and provide relevant context.
  • Calendar & Gmail informationAnswer requests using connections enabled in Settings.
  • Saved memoryPersonal facts, preferences or suggested patterns used to personalise replies, where configured.
The person who administers the home computer may be able to access files on it. Use a server and administrator you trust.

Family profiles separate the app experience. Child-mode review controls and access depend on the installed version and its settings.

04

Connected services

Local storage does not prevent a connected provider from processing information elsewhere. Providers may process information outside your country. Their terms, data controls and retention policies apply. Phone-access services may also process connection information where enabled.

Google & Gmail data+

Gmail access is optional and uses the read-only Gmail permission. Depending on Settings, Nemo can keep local email snippets or retrieve relevant information for a request. Relevant results may be included in AI context.

Nemo’s use of information received from Google APIs must adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy ↗, including the Limited Use requirements ↗. This includes restricting transfers to permitted, consented uses and prohibiting use for advertising or general-purpose model training.

Public Google connections are not being offered by this announcement website. A public app rollout must confirm the selected AI provider’s data handling, the consent flow and applicable Google verification requirements. This page does not claim Google verification.

05

Your choices

Use the controls available in your installed app to review connections, tool access, history and memory. Disconnecting an account and deleting a local record are different actions.

Retention, deletion & backups+

For a self-hosted installation, the home-server operator controls local records and backups. Signing out does not by itself delete a person’s profile or conversation history.

Available removal controls act on records held by that installation. Original Google emails, exported files, backups and records held by an AI provider can remain under separate retention rules. Ask the server operator or the relevant provider to handle those copies.

There is no single project-wide retention period for every independently hosted installation. Check the version and configuration you use.

For access, correction or deletion requests, or a privacy complaint about website correspondence, contact dan.zheng@danos.com.au. For app records, start with the operator of your home server; the public Nemo website cannot retrieve those records.

If you raise a privacy concern, tell us what happened and how to reach you. Where applicable, you can also contact a privacy regulator such as the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner ↗.

06

Children & early access

The current app preview is limited to invited adult testers aged 18 and over. Public access for children, including Child mode, is pending provider-eligibility and safety review. Child-mode examples on this website illustrate Nemo’s family vision; they are not an invitation for children to sign up.

Child mode is intended to guide learning. It does not guarantee that every unsuitable answer or workaround will be prevented. Families should understand the parent-review controls and use an AI connection permitted for the intended age group.

We will update this notice when the preview or its data practices change. Review it again before connecting new services or moving to a public app release.
