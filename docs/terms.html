01

The early preview

Nemo is an independent project built around Hermes Agent. The public website introduces a Windows development preview; it is not an app sign-up or a promise of a public release date.

Examples use fictional data. Shared family-calendar planning is a future feature, with each person choosing what to share. Calendar and Gmail are optional. Automatic Memory and local AI models are deferred from v1.

02

Responsible use

Use Nemo only for lawful purposes and with permission to access the accounts and information you connect. Do not use it to invade someone’s privacy, bypass access controls or cause harm.

AI responses can be inaccurate, incomplete or unsuitable. Check important dates, instructions and factual claims. Review any action before relying on it.

Nemo is an AI tool. It is not an emergency service or a substitute for qualified professional advice.
03

Family use

The current app preview is limited to invited adult testers aged 18 and over. Public access for children, including Child mode, is pending provider-eligibility and safety review. Child-mode examples on this website illustrate Nemo’s family vision; they are not an invitation for children to sign up.

Child mode aims to encourage understanding rather than complete homework for a child. It is an evolving feature, with limitations. Parent controls do not guarantee that every unsafe response or workaround will be caught.

Parents and guardians should understand what is stored, what they can review, and which connected tools are enabled. Explain those choices to the child in language they can understand.

04

Connected services

You are responsible for using an eligible AI account or plan and following that service’s terms, age rules, usage limits and payment requirements. Connecting a parent’s account does not by itself establish permission for every family member to use it.

Google, AI providers and any enabled phone-access services operate under their own terms. Nemo’s software licence does not grant rights to those services or their data. See how connected information is handled.
