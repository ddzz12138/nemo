A few good questions.

When can I try it?+

There’s no launch date yet. We’re testing with family before an invited Windows alpha. Public app source and setup instructions will accompany the first release. macOS support needs validation.

Is Nemo free?+

Nemo’s software will be free under the MIT license. You provide the computer and compatible Hermes setup. Your chosen AI provider may charge for usage.

What stays on my computer?+

Profiles, chats, and GBrain memory are stored at home. Replies use the model provider configured in Hermes. A cloud provider receives conversation context and enabled tool results; hosting Nemo locally doesn’t make every AI request local.

Can everyone see my conversations?+

Adult profiles have separate chats, memory, and accounts. Your other devices can resume your profile. Parents can review child conversations and memory, and that review is visible to the child.

Will it do my child’s homework?+

Child mode offers hints and explanations to help children learn. Parents choose which tools are available. It’s an early feature, and we’ll keep improving it.

Can I use it away from home?+

Yes, through a private HTTPS connection such as Tailscale, while your computer stays awake and online. Nemo opens in your phone’s browser and can be added to the home screen. This public website never accesses your family’s server.
